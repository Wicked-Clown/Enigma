The Bespoke Process

From Vision
to Reality

Creating a bespoke piece is a deeply personal process. We guide you through every step — from the initial concept to the moment you hold the finished piece in your hands.

Step 01

Initial Consultation

We begin with a conversation — in person at Stoke Jewellers or via WhatsApp. You share your vision, budget, and timeline.

Step 02

Design & Proposal

We present a detailed design concept with stone selection and pricing. You approve every element before a single piece of gold is touched.

Step 03

Skilled Crafting

Your piece is crafted by hand. Each diamond is set with precision. The gold is worked to your exact specification.

Step 04

Final Delivery

Collect in person from Stoke Jewellers in Coventry. Every piece leaves with full certification and our guarantee of quality.
